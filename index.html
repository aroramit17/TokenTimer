Privacy policy

Private by default

TokenTimer runs in your browser. Timers, theme, display mode, tab preferences, and accent choices are saved only in this browser's local storage. The site does not require an account, does not send pasted reset text to a server, and does not sell or share personal data.

If you clear browser storage, your saved timers and preferences are removed. Sharing uses your browser's built-in share sheet when available, or copies the site link to your clipboard.
